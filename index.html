Music Room

A small practice room with a weighted-key piano, a drum kit, an acoustic guitar and a lever harp. Everything is played with your bare hands: press the keys, strike the drums, sweep across the strings. The tape deck on the side table records your session so you can hear it back. Nothing is saved.

Headset: press Enter VR below. Pinch empty air and pull sideways to swivel your seat.
Desktop: click anywhere to start. Click keys and drums, drag across the guitar or harp strings, drag empty space to look around.
