Music Room

A quiet music room at night: a wood fire in the hearth, candles, plants, and a starry sky over the hills outside the window. A weighted-key piano with a working sustain pedal, a drum kit, an acoustic guitar and a lever harp, all played with your bare hands. The tape deck on the side table records your session so you can hear it back. Nothing is saved.

Headset: choose Enter VR room for the full room, or Play in my room to see your real space and place the piano on your own table or floor. In the room, pinch empty air and pull sideways to swivel; pinch near an instrument and drag to pull it closer.
Desktop: click anywhere to start. Drag to look all around the room, scroll to zoom. Click or sweep across the piano keys, or play them from your keyboard (A S D F row, Z and X for octaves, Space for sustain). Click drums, drag across guitar or harp strings. Shift+drag an instrument to move it. Fire, wind and rain switches sit on the side table.
